About Us

About ONYX SPA DARJEELING

ONYX SPA DARJEELING is a boutique wellness spa located inside Orsino Spa Resort in Gymkhana, Mall Road, Darjeeling. We offer a relaxing environment where guests can disconnect from the pace of travel and enjoy professionally delivered spa and wellness experiences.

Our spa welcomes both in-house hotel guests and outside guests visiting Darjeeling. Whether you are looking to unwind after sightseeing, relax after a long journey, or simply enjoy some personal wellness time, ONYX SPA DARJEELING provides a peaceful space dedicated to relaxation and rejuvenation.
